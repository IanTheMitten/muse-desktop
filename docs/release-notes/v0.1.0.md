# Muse 0.1.0 (alpha)

Muse is a local music player and recommender for your own music folders. No
account, no server.

## What it does

- Plays the folders you point it at.
- Recommends what to play next from audio, lyrics, emotion and your taste.
- Shows lyrics, and times them to the audio with auto-align.
- Fetches cover art.

Smart features (listening to songs and reading lyrics on your computer) are an
optional download in Settings. The models are free for non-commercial use only,
so Muse is free and for non-commercial use.

## Install

The builds are unsigned, so the first launch needs one extra step:

- **macOS:** right-click the app and choose Open.
- **Windows:** in the SmartScreen warning choose More info, then Run anyway.

Intel Macs get the app but no smart features.

## Known gaps

- Media keys and the Now Playing widget are unverified.
- Some online sources may be unavailable.
