# Music in the README

The screenshots and the demo GIF use these freely licensed recordings and
their cover art. `scripts/readme_shots.py` downloads them, tags them and
embeds the covers; nothing here is bundled with Muse.

## The Wake, Josh Woodward (2012)

[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Source: <https://joshwoodward.bandcamp.com/album/the-wake>

Tracks used: Crazy Glue, Golden Sunrise, Lafayette, I Will Not Let You Let Me Down.

## The Adventure Goes On, Vol. 1, Komiku (2020)

[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Source: <https://archive.org/details/komiku-the-adventure-goes-on-vol.-1>

Cover: Vincent van Gogh, The Sower (1888), public domain.

Tracks used: The calling, Tonight, we feast !, Sweet night and friends.

## Reappear, Chris Zabriskie (2012)

[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Source: <https://chriszabriskie.bandcamp.com/album/reappear>

Tracks used: Readers! Do You Read?, Is That You or Are You You?, Virtues Inherited, Vices Passed On.

## Dunes at Night, Ozzed (2017)

[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Source: <https://ozzed.bandcamp.com/album/dunes-at-night>

Tracks used: Frozen Langos, Blueberry March, Dunes at Night.

## Lyrics

`lyrics/josh-woodward-crazy-glue.lrc`: the lyrics of Crazy Glue by Josh
Woodward (CC BY 4.0, <https://www.joshwoodward.com/song/CrazyGlue>), with line
timings added for this README. The covers appear as published; Muse's
colour wash around them is generated from them at runtime.
